Privacy policy: rclone-wsl-backup

rclone-wsl-backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, which gives access only to files the tool itself creates.
  • Files are copied from the owner's computer directly to the owner's Google Drive. No data is sent to any other server.
  • No personal data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
